escrow, plainly

Nobody’s money moves until the contest is over

Holding other people’s prize money is serious, so the flow is boring on purpose.

Host funds the pool

A paid tournament can’t open registration until the full prize pool clears through Razorpay.

Players pay entry

Each fee is captured and held in escrow by the platform. Entry fees carry no platform fee.

Results lock

The host confirms, or 24 hours pass with no dispute. Only then are ranks fixed.

Winners get paid

Automatic payouts to a verified UPI ID or bank account, after the 1% pool fee.

Refunds are real

Cancel before start, or miss the minimum players, and every entry fee and the whole pool go back in full. A player eliminated for cheating gets no refund — that’s the point.

Webcam + tab-lock

  • Camera check before you can start; small corner preview the whole match.
  • A snapshot every 20–30 seconds, visible only to the host and admins.
  • Switch tab, minimise, or leave fullscreen and the match locks — for good.
  • The lock lives on the server, so a refresh won’t bring you back.
honest bit

We stop casual cheating. We can’t stop all of it.

A second device or a second person off-camera will beat any webcam. So we layer the checks and keep the host’s right to eliminate as the final word.

What we actually do

Snapshots on a timer, similarity checks between submissions in the same room, one active session per account, a 30-second grace period for dropped connections, and a violation log the host can review before eliminating anyone. The rules page says all of this out loud before you pay.
